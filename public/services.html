Care built around you

Every treatment plan is individualized. Naturopathic visits take place by video and are available to patients across Ontario.

Naturopathic medicine consult

Naturopathic medicine is a holistic approach to health that focuses on the root causes of illness rather than just treating symptoms. Dr. Averbuch looks at the interplay between your lifestyle, genetics, environment and lab results, then combines evidence-based natural therapies such as clinical nutrition, herbal medicine, acupuncture and lifestyle changes into a personalized plan that targets both root causes and symptoms.

Functional & laboratory testing

Constantly bloated? Chronically fatigued? Acne that won't go away? “Test, don't guess” is Dr. Averbuch's motto. Laboratory testing gives an in-depth look at what's happening internally, so you both have a clear understanding of the root cause of your symptoms.

Nutrition & lifestyle counselling

Food is a cornerstone of optimal health. Dr. Averbuch builds a personalized nutrition plan around your needs and goals, and focuses on teaching habits you can sustain, which is the key to lasting change.

Supplement guidance

Supplements can be stressful and confusing. Dr. Averbuch takes the guesswork away with individualized recommendations that address the root cause of your specific conditions and deficiencies.

Hands-on treatment

Traditional Chinese Medicine acupuncture

Acupuncture works by promoting the flow of Qi throughout the body, helping to clear energy blockages that may be hindering your health. Each treatment is customized to your symptoms and a personalized Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) assessment. Acupuncture can be effective for a variety of concerns, including digestive issues, fertility optimization, fatigue, anxiety, pain and insomnia.

Hands-on treatment

Therapeutic cupping

Small cups create gentle to moderate suction on the skin and muscles to break up stagnation, increase blood flow, ease tension and promote muscle relaxation. Using fixed and gliding techniques, cupping can support concerns such as chronic pain, lymphatic drainage and facial rejuvenation.

Acupuncture and cupping are hands-on treatments. Get in touch to ask about in-person availability.

Common questions

Before you book

Are appointments virtual?

Yes. Naturopathic visits take place by video and are available to patients anywhere in Ontario. Acupuncture and cupping are hands-on treatments, so get in touch to ask about in-person availability.

How do I get started?

Book a free 15-minute meet & greet. You'll outline your health concerns and learn how naturopathic care can help. New patients then book a 75-minute initial assessment.

What happens at the initial assessment?

An in-depth conversation about your health history, current concerns and goals, with lab testing if indicated. You'll leave with your initial treatment plan.

Is naturopathic care covered by insurance?

Many extended health benefit plans in Canada include naturopathic care. Check your plan to see what it covers.

Does Dr. Averbuch see children?

Yes. Visits for infants and children are booked directly with Dr. Averbuch. Mention it during your free meet & greet or send her an email.

Is Dr. Averbuch a registered naturopathic doctor?

Yes. She is registered with, and in good standing with, the College of Naturopaths of Ontario, which regulates naturopaths in the province. She earned her Doctor of Naturopathy at the Canadian College of Naturopathic Medicine and is a member of the Ontario and Canadian associations of naturopathic doctors.

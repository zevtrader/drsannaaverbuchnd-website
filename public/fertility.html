Special interest

Naturopathic care for fertility

Trying for a while, living with irregular cycles, or planning ahead for a pregnancy? Dr. Averbuch, ND offers virtual naturopathic fertility support, by video anywhere in Ontario.

Who it's for

Dr. Averbuch, ND supports people who are:

  • Planning a pregnancy and want to prepare
  • Trying to conceive for a while, including with unexplained infertility
  • Living with irregular cycles
  • Living with PCOS or endometriosis
  • Coping with recurrent pregnancy loss
  • Going through IVF or IUI and looking for naturopathic support alongside it

How Dr. Averbuch, ND approaches fertility

Dr. Averbuch, ND looks at your cycle, health history and lab work, then builds an individualized plan with you. Her approach combines evidence-based medicine with natural therapies such as clinical nutrition, supplementation, herbal medicine and lifestyle changes.

Depending on your history, lab work may include thyroid function, iron, vitamin D and hormone testing.

Your first visit

Your 75-minute initial assessment is an in-depth look at your health history, current concerns and goals, with lab testing if indicated. You'll leave with your initial treatment plan. Follow-up visits help monitor progress and adjust your plan.

Fees and insurance

The initial assessment is $250, by video. Many extended health plans cover naturopathic care, and you'll receive a receipt to submit to your plan. See all visits and fees.

Common questions

Fertility questions

Can I see Dr. Averbuch, ND while going through IVF or IUI?

Yes. Naturopathic care can be used alongside IVF or IUI. Dr. Averbuch, ND doesn't work directly with fertility clinics, so please let your fertility team know you're seeing a naturopathic doctor.

Do you see partners?

Yes. Partners, including male partners, can book their own initial assessment for support with their fertility health.

What lab work might be requested?

It depends on your history. Lab work may include thyroid function, iron, vitamin D and hormone testing.

Are visits virtual?

Yes. Visits take place by video, anywhere in Ontario.

Is it covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans cover naturopathic care. You'll receive a receipt to submit to your plan.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book online directly.

Naturopathic care is meant to complement, not replace, care from your family doctor or fertility specialist.

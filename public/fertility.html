Special interest

Naturopathic care for fertility

Virtual naturopathic support for people who are trying to conceive, by video anywhere in Ontario.

Who it's for

Dr. Averbuch, ND supports people who are:

  • Planning a pregnancy and want to prepare
  • Trying to conceive for a while, including with unexplained infertility
  • Living with irregular cycles
  • Living with PCOS or endometriosis
  • Coping with recurrent pregnancy loss

How Dr. Averbuch, ND approaches fertility

Dr. Averbuch, ND looks at your cycle, health history and lab work, then builds an individualized plan with you. Her approach combines evidence-based medicine with natural therapies such as clinical nutrition, supplementation, herbal medicine and lifestyle changes.

Your first visit

Your 75-minute initial assessment is an in-depth look at your health history, current concerns and goals, with lab testing if indicated. You'll leave with your initial treatment plan. Follow-up visits help monitor progress and adjust your plan.

Fees and insurance

The initial assessment is $250, by video. Many extended health plans cover naturopathic care, and you'll receive a receipt to submit to your plan. See all visits and fees.

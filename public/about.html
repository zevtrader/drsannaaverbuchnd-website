My journey to naturopathic medicine has been a special one. Growing up with a mother who practised as a naturopathic doctor for over 24 years, I was fortunate to have naturopathic principles woven into my life from a young age, shaping my health and wellness in such a positive way.

I also experienced my own health challenges, particularly with dermatology and women's health, and saw firsthand the profound impact naturopathic medicine can have on well-being. That experience deepened my appreciation for this unique and transformative approach to healing.

My approach to care

As a naturopathic doctor, I take a patient-centred approach: I take the time to understand every aspect of your life and create an individualized treatment plan tailored to your goals, because each person is unique. My approach combines evidence-based medicine with natural therapies such as clinical nutrition, supplementation, herbal medicine, acupuncture and lifestyle modifications.

I focus on uncovering the root cause of your health concerns through comprehensive assessments, lab work, a close look at your symptoms and physical exams.

I'm passionate about empowering my patients through education, guiding you not only to heal but to master your own health journey. In a world full of conflicting advice, I prioritize the latest evidence-based guidance, and use blood work where appropriate, to help you make informed decisions about diet, supplements and lifestyle.

Our work together begins with an in-depth initial assessment, where we explore your health as a whole, not just your symptoms. From there, we design a comprehensive plan with personalized recommendations that are realistic to implement. Follow-up visits provide ongoing support as needed, so we can monitor progress, adjust your plan and stay on track toward your long-term goals.

Special interests

  • Fertility: support for people trying to conceive, with a close look at your cycle, health history and lab work, and an individualized plan built with you
  • Women's health & hormone imbalances: menstrual irregularities, PMS, menopause
  • Dermatology: acne, eczema, psoriasis, facial rejuvenation
  • Digestive concerns: bloating, IBS, GERD, bowel changes
  • Stress management & chronic fatigue

Education

I began my path to becoming a naturopathic doctor at Wilfrid Laurier University, where I earned a BA Honours in Psychology. I then completed an additional four years of naturopathic medical training at the Canadian College of Naturopathic Medicine (CCNM), where I obtained my Doctor of Naturopathy degree.

During my clinical training, I took part in the Reproductive Focus Shift and completed a one-year internship at the Robert Schad Naturopathic Clinic.

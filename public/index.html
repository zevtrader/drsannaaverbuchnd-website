Common questions

Before you book

Can a naturopathic doctor help if I'm trying to conceive?

Dr. Averbuch supports people who are planning a pregnancy, have irregular cycles, PCOS or endometriosis, have experienced recurrent pregnancy loss, or have been trying for a while. She looks at your cycle, health history and lab work, then builds an individualized plan with you.

Are visits virtual?

Yes. Visits take place by video, anywhere in Ontario. For acupuncture or cupping in person, email Dr. Averbuch to ask about availability.

What happens at the first visit?

Your 75-minute initial assessment is an in-depth look at your health history, current concerns and goals, with lab testing if indicated. You'll leave with your initial treatment plan.

Is it covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans cover naturopathic care. You'll receive a receipt to submit to your plan.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book online directly.

Meet Dr. Averbuch

Rooted in naturopathic medicine from a young age

Today she combines evidence-based medicine with natural therapies to uncover the root cause of your symptoms.
